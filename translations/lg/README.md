# Okuyiga Okukozesa JW Library

Ekitabo ekiyamba okutendeka n'enteekateeka ya kiraasi, ebyetongodde era ebyangu okukozesa, ebyategekebwa okuyamba abantu okuyiga engeri y'okukozesa pulogulaamu ya **JW Library** mu kusoma Bayibuli ku lwabwe, mu nkuŋŋaana z'ekibiina, ne mu buweereza.

Okuyiga kuno kutegekeddwa okuba **kiraasi ya ddakiika 60** etali ya kanyigo, nga mulimu **eddakiika 10 ez'okufundikira**, era kusobola okwongezebwako okutuuka ku **ddakiika 75–90** eri ebibinja ebyagala okufuna ekiseera ekisingawo eky'okwegezaamu.

> **Ebikwata ku nkyusa eno:** Eno nkyusa ya Luganda ey'ekitabo ekyasooka okuwandiikibwa mu Lungereza. Amannya g'obupeesa mu pulogulaamu gawandiikiddwa mu Luganda, ate erinnya ery'Olungereza ne liteekebwa mu bukomera, kubanga ebigambo by'olaba ku ssirini yo bisinziira ku lulimi kyuma kyo lwe kikozesa.

## 🌐 Ekitabo eky'oku Mukutu ky'Osobola Okukozesa

Enkyusa ey'oku mukutu ey'okuyiga kuno—ekola obulungi ku ssirini za buli bunene, erimu ebiragiro ebisinziira ku kyuma ky'olonze, era ng'ennukuta nnene—esangibwa wano:

**[https://neiltucker.github.io/JWLibrary/](https://neiltucker.github.io/JWLibrary/)** *(mu Lungereza)*

Ekitabo ky'oku mukutu kirimu:
* **Okulonda Ekyuma:** Londa **Apple (iOS / iPadOS)**, **Android**, oba **Kompyuta ya Windows / Mac** olabe amannya g'obupeesa n'engeri menu gye zitegekeddwamu ku kyuma kyo.
* **Ennukuta Ennene n'Obupeesa Obukyusa Obunene bw'Ennukuta:** Kitegekeddwa okusomebwa mu ngeri ennyangu, era osobola okukendeeza oba okwongera ku bunene bw'ennukuta (`A−`, `A`, `A+`, `A++`).
* **Enkola y'Ekizikiza n'Enkola y'Ekitangaala:** Eppeesa limu waggulu okusoma nga totawaanya maaso mu kifo ekirimu ekitangaala ekitono.
* **Ekitabo Ekimpi Ekisobola Okukubibwa mu Kyapa:** Tegeka era okube empapula ennyonjo ez'okujjukiza abayizi nga beegezaamu.
* **Ebiragiro Ebimpi Ebikulu:** Emitendera egitegeerekeka obulungi egirina ennamba 1-2-3 wamu n'obubonero (📖, 📚, 📅, ☀️, 🔍, 🔖, 🖍️, 🎧, 💾).

## 📋 Ebikwata ku Masomo mu Bufunze

Amasomo gategekeddwa mu masomo asatu, buli limu lya ddakiika 15, nga gasookebwako okuyambibwa okw'eddakiika 15 nga kiraasi tennatandika, eddakiika 5 ez'okwaniriza, n'eddakiika 10 ez'okufundikira:

0. **Okuyambibwa nga Kiraasi Tennatandika n'Okumanyiira (Eddakiika 5–15)**
   * **Okuyambibwa kw'Abo Abatuuka Kare (eddakiika 15 nga tennatandika):** Abayambi babaniriza abazze, bayunga ebyuma byabwe ku Wi-Fi y'ekifo, era bakakasa nti Bayibuli n'Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku ekiriwo biwanuddwa nga kiraasi tennatandika.
   * **Okukebera mu Sikonda 30 oba Abantu Beetegese:** Abayizi bayimusa emikono mangu abayambi basobole okuyamba oyo yenna akyetaaga okuwanula Bayibuli nga kiraasi etandika.
   * **Emirembe mu Mutima ("Eppeesa ery'Akabenje"):** Okuyigiriza abayizi engeri y'okusirisa amangu eddoboozi eritasuubiddwa nga bakozesa **eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi** *(Volume Down)* eriri ku mabbali g'ekyuma (ku ssimu/tabuleti), **eppeesa erisirisa (🔇)** *(Mute)* ku kiwandiikiro kya kompyuta, oba **Yimiriza (⏸️)** *(Pause)* ku ssirini.
   * Okuzuula olukalala olukulu olw'okutambuliramu (navigation bar) n'okutereeza ekiseera ssirini mw'eggalirwa / mw'ezikira.

1. **Essomo 1: Okuzuula Ebyawandiikibwa n'Enkuŋŋaana (Eddakiika 15)**
   * Okwetegereza olukalala olukulu olw'okutambuliramu n'okusoma **Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku** *(Daily Text)* eky'olwaleero.
   * Okutereeza **Obunene bw'Ennukuta (`A A`)** osobole okusoma bulungi awatali kufuba.
   * Okuggulawo **ekitabo n'essuula ya Bayibuli**.
   * Okukozesa **ekitundu ky'Enkuŋŋaana** *(Meetings)* okuggulawo omulundi gumu akatabo k'Olukuŋŋaana olw'Omu Wiiki n'ekitundu eky'okusoma mu *Omunaala gw'Omukuumi* eky'Olukuŋŋaana olwa Wikendi.
   * *Omusomesa Alaga:* Obubonero bw'okujjukira *(Bookmarks)*, Ebyafaayo *(History)*, ne Noonya *(Search)*.

2. **Essomo 2: Okuwanula n'Okukozesa Amaloboozi ne Vidiyo (Eddakiika 15)**
   * Okuyunga ku Wi-Fi nga tonnawanula, osobole okukekkereza data y'essimu.
   * Okuwanula ebitabo oba amaloboozi ne vidiyo okubikozesa nga tolina yintaneeti (bw'omala okubiwanula, teweetaaga yintaneeti mu nkuŋŋaana).
   * Okukozesa **Ekizannya Amaloboozi (🎧)** *(Audio Player)* okuwuliriza essuula za Bayibuli, Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku, oba ebitundu eby'okusoma nga bisomebwa mu ddoboozi.
   * **Okusirisa Amangu mu Kabenje:** Okukomya eddoboozi amangu ddala ng'okozesa obupeesa bw'eddoboozi bw'ekyuma (okunyweza eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi eriri ku mabbali g'essimu; okunyiga eppeesa erisirisa ku kiwandiikiro kya kompyuta) oba Yimiriza ku ssirini.
   * *Omusomesa Alaga:* Okukebera ekifo eky'okuterekamu n'okusengeka ng'osinziira ku **Ekisinga Obunene** *(Largest Size)* okuggyawo fayiro ezitali nneetaagisa.

3. **Essomo 3: Ebikozesebwa mu Kwesomesa (Eddakiika 15)**
   * Okussaako langi ya kyenvu ku butundu obw'okusoma ne ku nnyiriri za Bayibuli.
   * Okukyusa langi (okugeza, okugifuula bbulu oba kiragala) oba okuggyawo langi.
   * **Okukuuma Ebyo by'Owandiise:** Okukola fayiro y'ekkopi (`.jwlibrary`) mu *Entegeka > Okutereka Kkopi n'Okugizzaawo* *(Settings > Backup and Restore)* ebiwandiiko byo n'obubonero bw'okusoma bireme kubula n'akatono.
   * *Omusomesa Alaga:* Okwongerako ebiwandiiko, okusengeka ng'okozesa *Tags*, n'okukozesa ekitundu ekikulu eky'Okwesomesa *(Personal Study)*.

4. **Okufundikira, Okuddamu Okwetegereza, n'Ebibuuzo (Eddakiika 10)**
   * Eddakiika 10 ezaateekebwawo ez'okuddamu okwetegereza, ebibuuzo, oba ekiseera eky'enyongeza singa okwegezaamu okwasooka kutwala ekiseera ekisingawo.
   * Abakulembera kiraasi basobola okusigalawo oluvannyuma lwa kiraasi okuyamba oyo yenna ayagala okuyambibwa ng'ali yekka.
   * *Amagezi eri Akulembera Kiraasi:* Teeka kkopi z'Enteekateeka ya Kiraasi ey'olupapula olumu ezikubiddwa mu kyapa ku mmeeza ng'awali yintaneeti enafu oba ey'ebbeeyi, kozesa obukadi bwa QR ku mmeeza abayizi baggulewo ekitabo kino nga bakozesa kamera y'essimu, era olage abayizi "Add to Home Screen" ekitabo kisobole okugguka ne bwe waba tewali yintaneeti.

## 📱 Ebyuma Ebikozesebwa n'Ebyetaagisa

Okukakasa nti ebipya n'ebirongoosa eby'obukuumi bifunika, kakasa nti ekyuma kyo kituukana n'ebyetaagisa ebitono ebya sisitimu ebyateekebwawo ku lwa pulogulaamu eno:

| Sisitimu | Enkyusa Esookerwako Ekozesebwa |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Android** | Android 8.0 oba empya okusingawo |
| **Apple iOS / iPadOS** | iOS 15.0 oba empya okusingawo |
| **Mac** | macOS erina Apple Silicon (chip ya M1 oba empya okusingawo) |
| **Windows** | Windows 10 Version 2004 oba empya okusingawo |

### Bye Tukuwa Amagezi Okukola nga Tonnajja:
* **Tuuka Kare (Eddakiika 10–15 nga Tekinnatuuka):** Tuuka eddakiika ntono nga bukyali abayambi basobole okukuyunga ku Wi-Fi y'ekifo n'okukakasa nti Bayibuli yo n'Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku byetegefu.
* **Bbaatule Ejjudde:** Leeta ekyuma kyo nga bbaatule ejjudde (oba leeta waya yo ecaajinga).
* **Okutereeza Ekiseera Ssirini mw'Ezikira:** Lowooza ku ky'okuteekawo okumala akaseera ssirini y'ekyuma kyo eggalirwe oba ezikire oluvannyuma lw'eddakiika 2 oba 5, ssirini ereme kuzikira ng'abantu balagibwa.
* **Pulogulaamu Eteekeddwamu:** Teeka pulogulaamu ya *JW Library* ku kyuma kyo okuva mu dduuka lya pulogulaamu ery'omutindo (app store) nga kiraasi tennatandika.
* **Ekifo eky'Okuterekamu:** Kakasa nti ekyuma kyo kirina waakiri **GB 2 okutuuka ku 4** ez'ekifo ekitaliiko kintu (oba SD card eteekeddwamu) osobole okuwanula ebitabo, amaloboozi, ne vidiyo awatali buzibu.
* **Okuyunga ku Wi-Fi:** Yunga ku Wi-Fi eriwo nga tonnawanula fayiro ennene ez'amaloboozi ne vidiyo osobole okukekkereza data y'essimu.
* **Amagezi ag'Okusirisa mu Kabenje:** Jjukira nti okunyiga **eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi** (ku ssimu/tabuleti) oba **eppeesa erisirisa (🔇)** ku kiwandiikiro (ku laputopu) kijja kusirisa amangu eddoboozi singa livaayo nga terisuubiddwa.

## 📁 Ebiri mu Tterekero Lino

* **`lg.html`**: Okuyiga kw'oku mukutu okujjuvu mu Luganda, okwekyusa okusinziira ku ssirini, okulimu menu ey'okulonda olulimi, obupeesa obulonda ekyuma (ekyuma ky'olonze kijjukirwa), okukyusa obunene bw'ennukuta, ekitabo ky'ekiseera eky'akulembera kiraasi, n'ekitabo ekimpi ekisobola okukubibwa mu kyapa.
  *Teeka `lg.html` mu folda y'emu ne `index.html` (Olungereza), `sw.html`, ne `din.html` menu y'olulimi esobole okukola.*
* **`manifest-lg.json` ne `sw.js`**: Fayiro ezisobozesa ekitabo kino okuteekebwa ku ssirini ey'omu maaso ey'essimu n'okugguka mu Luganda. Omuyizi bw'amala okuggulawo ekitabo kino omulundi gumu nga waliwo yintaneeti, empapula ennya ez'ekitabo kino ziterekebwa ku kyuma ne zigguka nga tewali yintaneeti. PDF, vidiyo, ne link za JW.ORG bikyetaaga yintaneeti.
* **`Table_Cards_Luganda_lg.pdf`**: Obukadi bwa QR obw'okukuba mu kyapa (obukadi 4 ku lupapula lumu) obuggulawo `lg.html`.
* **`QR_Luganda_lg.png` ne `QR_Luganda_lg.svg`**: Koodi ya QR eggulawo olupapula olw'Oluganda (PNG ya ssirini, SVG ya kukuba mu kyapa).
* **`JWLibrary_Class.md`**: Enteekateeka y'amasomo mu Markdown erimu engeri ekiseera gye kigabanyizibwamu eri omusomesa, amagezi eri akulembera kiraasi, n'okwegezaamu okukulu okw'abayizi.
* **`JW Library Class Program.pdf`**: Enteekateeka ya kiraasi ey'olupapula 1 esobola okukubibwa mu kyapa n'olupapula olujjukiza okwegezaamu *(mu Lungereza mu tterekero eryasooka)*.
* **`JW Library Tutorial Curriculum.pdf`**: Enteekateeka y'amasomo enzijuvu ey'empapula 4 n'ekitabo ekiyamba abasomesa *(mu Lungereza mu tterekero eryasooka)*.
* **`generate_pdfs.py`**: Pulogulaamu ya Python ekozesa ReportLab okuddamu okukola PDF ezo. Mu nkyusa eno, ekola PDF za Luganda (`Enteekateeka ya Kiraasi ya JW Library.pdf` ne `Enteekateeka y'Amasomo ga JW Library.pdf`) awatali kusangulawo PDF ez'Olungereza.
* **`tinyurl.txt`**: Link empi ey'okuyitamu.
