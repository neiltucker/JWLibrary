## 🧑‍🏫 Ebiyamba Akulembera Kiraasi n'Omusomesa

* **Okuyambibwa nga Kiraasi Tennatandika (Eddakiika 15 nga Bukyali):** Langirira nti enzigi zijja kuggulwawo eddakiika 15 nga kiraasi tennatandika. Abayambi baniriza abazze ku mulyango, bayunge ebyuma byabwe ku Wi-Fi y'ekifo, era bakakase nti **Bayibuli** n'**Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku** ekiriwo biwanuddwa abayizi basobole okutandika Essomo 1 awatali buzibu bwa tekinologiya.
* **Abayambi ku Mmeeza:** Funa **abayambi 1 oba 2** abanaatambulatambula mu kisenge mpola ng'abayizi beegezaamu. Omuyizi bw'ava mu pulogulaamu mu butanwa oba ssirini ye bw'eggalawo, omuyambi asobola okumusemberera n'amuzzaayo mu kifo ekituufu mu sikonda 5 awatali kuyimiriza kiraasi.
* **Obulagirizi ku Kusirisa mu Kabenje ("Eppeesa ery'Akabenje"):** Abantu abatamanyidde tekinologiya n'abakadde batera okutya ennyo nti amaloboozi oba vidiyo bisobola okuvaayo nga tebisuubiddwa era n'eddoboozi ery'omwanguka mu nkuŋŋaana oba mu kiraasi. Nga temunnakwata ku bintu by'amaloboozi ne vidiyo, yigiriza abayizi "buleeki ey'akabenje" etuukirawo ku kyuma kyabwe:
  * **Essimu ne Tabuleti (Apple / Android):** Nyiga era onyweze **eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi** *(Volume Down)* eriri ku mabbali g'ekyuma, oba nyiga **Yimiriza (⏸️)** *(Pause)* ku ssirini.
  * **Kompyuta (Windows / Mac):** Nyiga **eppeesa erisirisa (🔇)** *(Mute)* oba **eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi** ku kiwandiikiro (ebiseera ebisinga liri ku lunyiriri lw'obupeesa olwa waggulu), nyiga akabonero ka sipiika ku taskbar/menu bar, oba nyiga **Yimiriza (⏸️)**.
* **Okukebera Ekiseera Ssirini mw'Ezikira:** Ku ntandikwa ya kiraasi, bawe amagezi okuteekawo okumala akaseera ssirini y'ekyuma kyabwe eggalirwe oba ezikire oluvannyuma lw'**eddakiika 2 oba 5**, ssirini ereme kuzikira nga bagoberera.
* **Eddakiika 10 ez'Enyongeza:** Okwegezaamu bwe kusukka eddakiika 15, kwata mpola eddakiika 3–5 okuva mu ddakiika 10 ez'okufundikira awatali kwanguyiriza.
* **Okuyambibwa Oluvannyuma lwa Kiraasi:** Langirira ku ntandikwa nti omusomesa n'abayambi bajja kusigalawo **eddakiika 15 oluvannyuma lwa kiraasi** okuyamba oyo yenna ayagala okuyambibwa ng'ali yekka mu ngeri etali ya kanyigo.
* **Kkopi Ezikubiddwa mu Kyapa (Bwe kiba kyetaagisa):** Okussa kkopi z'Enteekateeka ya Kiraasi ey'olupapula olumu ku mmeeza kisobozesa abayizi okugoberera emitendera gyonna egy'okwegezaamu mbagirawo, awatali kwetaaga kuggulawo kitabo kya ku mukutu ku ddata ey'essimu.
* **Koodi za QR ku Mmeeza oba ku Ssirini (Si kya buwaze):** Okussa koodi ya QR ku ssirini oba okuteeka obukadi bwa QR obukubiddwa mu kyapa (`Table_Cards_Luganda_lg.pdf`) ku mmeeza kiyamba abayizi okuggulawo ekitabo kino mbagirawo nga bakozesa kamera y'essimu yaabwe awatali kuwandiika linnya lya mukutu.
* **Yongereza ku Ssirini Ey'omu Maaso (Amagezi g'Okukozesa nga Tewali Yintaneeti):** Laga abayizi engeri y'okukozesa "Share > Add to Home Screen" (iPhone) oba "Menu > Add to Home screen" (Android; kiyinza okulabika nga "Install app"). Oluvannyuma buli muyizi aggulewo akabonero ako akapya omulundi gumu nga akyalina yintaneeti. Okuva olwo, empapula z'ekitabo kino zijja kugguka ne bwe waba tewali yintaneeti (PDF, vidiyo, ne link za JW.ORG bikyetaaga yintaneeti).

## Ebikwata ku Masomo

### Okwaniriza n'Okumanyiira (0:00 – 0:05)
* **0:00** Yaniriza abayizi; kakasa nti buli muntu ayungiddwa ku Wi-Fi y'ekifo.
* **0:01** **Okukebera mu Kulaba oba Abantu Beetegese:** Saba abayizi banyige **Bayibuli** (📖) *(Bible)*. Bwe balaba essuula (Olubereberye, Matayo), baba beetegese. Omuntu yenna bw'alaba akabonero k'ekire ak'okuwanula oba bw'aba yeetaaga obuyambi okuteeka Bayibuli oba Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku, ayimusa omukono, omuyambi n'amuyamba mpola mu kifo kye ng'atudde nga kiraasi etandika.
* **0:03** **Emirembe mu Mutima (Okusirisa mu Kabenje):** Laga engeri y'okusirisa amangu eddoboozi eritasuubiddwa: ku ssimu ne tabuleti, nyweza **eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi** eriri ku mabbali; ku laputopu ne kompyuta, nyiga **eppeesa erisirisa (🔇)** ku kiwandiikiro; oba nyiga **Yimiriza (⏸️)**.

## Okwegezaamu kw'Abayizi

### 1. Okwegezaamu Okuzuula Ebyawandiikibwa n'Enkuŋŋaana

* **Okukulu 1.1. Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku n'Obunene bw'Ennukuta:** Nyiga **Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku** (☀️) *(Daily Text)*. Ku lukalala olwa waggulu, nyiga akabonero **`A A`** era osereze akasaaliro ennukuta zifuuke nnene era nnyangu okusoma.
* **Okukulu 1.2. Essuula ya Bayibuli:** Nyiga **Bayibuli** (📖) *(Bible)*. Londa ekitabo (okugeza, *Matayo*) n'essuula (okugeza, *5*) otandike okusoma.
* **Okukulu 1.3. Ekitundu ky'Enkuŋŋaana:** Nyiga ekitundu ky'**Enkuŋŋaana** (📅) *(Meetings)*. Ggulawo butereevu ekitundu eky'okusoma mu *Omunaala gw'Omukuumi* ekya wiiki eno okuva ku nteekateeka y'olukuŋŋaana olwa wikendi.
* *Kirageddwa (Eky'enyongeza):* Kozesa akabonero k'**Okujjukira** (🔖) *(Bookmark)* okussa akabonero ku lunyiriri, akabonero k'**Ebyafaayo** (🕒) *(History)* okuddayo ku byawandiikibwa by'obadde osoma, oba akabonero ka **Noonya** (🔍) *(Search)* okunoonya ebigambo.

---

### 2. Okwegezaamu Okuwanula n'Okukozesa Amaloboozi ne Vidiyo

* **Okukulu 2.1. Okukebera Wi-Fi:** Kakasa nti ekyuma kyo kiyungiddwa ku Wi-Fi nga tonnatandika kuwanula kintu kyonna, osobole okukekkereza data y'essimu.
* **Okukulu 2.2. Okuwanula Okukozesa Awatali Yintaneeti:** Nyiga **Ebitabo** (📚) *(Publications)* oba enteekateeka y'olukuŋŋaana. Nyiga **akabonero k'Ekire (☁️)** ku kitabo eky'okusoma oba ku vidiyo empi okukiwanula okukikozesa awatali yintaneeti.
* **Okukulu 2.3. Okuwuliriza Amaloboozi n'Okusirisa Amangu:** Ggulawo Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku oba essuula ya Bayibuli. Nyiga **eppeesa lya Headphones / Zannya (🎧)** *(Play)* eriri waggulu ku ssirini owulirize okusoma. Weegezeemu okukomya eddoboozi amangu ddala: ku ssimu oba tabuleti, nyiga era onyweze **eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi** eriri ku mabbali; ku laputopu/kompyuta, nyiga **eppeesa erisirisa (🔇)** ku kiwandiikiro; oba nyiga **Yimiriza (⏸️)**.
* *Kirageddwa (Eky'enyongeza):* Mu **Ebitabo** > **Ebiwanuddwa** *(Publications > Downloaded)*, kyusa engeri gye bisengekeddwamu ofune **Ekisinga Obunene** *(Largest Size)* okukebera ekifo ekikozeseddwa n'okulaba engeri y'okusangulamu fayiro ezitali nneetaagisa.

---

### 3. Okwegezaamu Ebikozesebwa mu Kwesomesa

* **Okukulu 3.1. Ssaako Langi ku Kyokuddamu:** Mu kitundu eky'okusoma mu *Omunaala gw'Omukuumi* ekiriwo, nyiga era onyweze ekigambo ekisooka eky'ekyokuddamu mu katundu 1, walula engalo, olwo olonde **Kyenvu** *(Yellow)*.
* **Okukulu 3.2. Kyusa Langi:** Ddamu onyige ebigambo by'osizzaako langi. Londa langi endala (nga bbulu oba kiragala), oba nyiga akabonero ka kasasiro okuggyawo langi.
* **Okukulu 3.3. Kola Fayiro y'Ekkopi:** Nyiga **Entegeka** (⚙️) *(Settings)* > **Okutereka Kkopi n'Okugizzaawo** *(Backup and Restore)* > **Kola Kkopi** *(Create Backup)*. Tereka kkopi ya fayiro yo ey'okusoma (`.jwlibrary`) obubonero bwo obw'okusoma bureme kubula n'akatono.
* *Kirageddwa (Eky'enyongeza):* Nyiga ebigambo by'osizzaako langi, londa **Yongerako Ekiwandiiko** (📝) *(Add Note)*, era ogende mu kitundu ky'**Okwesomesa** *(Personal Study)* olabe ebiwandiiko byonna, obubonero bw'okujjukira, ne langi zonna mu kifo kimu.
