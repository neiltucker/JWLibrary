## 2. Wording to check first

**A. Not found in Dangme or in the Ga translation (the translator's own)**
1. **Silencing sound:** *Ha gbi ɔ nɛ kpa (amlɔ nɛ ɔ)* ("make the sound stop (now)"). Is *gbi* right for a phone's sound? Is *kpa* right for "stop"? Is there a more natural everyday way to say "silence the phone"?
2. **Press and hold (Volume Down button):** *Moo nyɛ … nɔ nɛ o kɛ o nine nɛ pi nɔ*.
3. **Wasting mobile data:** *konɛ o mobail data a ko puɛ*.
4. **Legal notice (exact meaning matters):** please compare it with the English original under it. *nɛ pi jua yemi ní tsumi* (non-commercial) and the negatives *a pee we, a ngmɛ we blɔ kaa a pee, a hyɛ we nɔ, a wo we he sika, nɛ a kplɛɛ we nɔ* are the translator's own. *be weku ko* for "not affiliated" follows Ga *wekukpaa* loosely.
5. **Small words from memory, not traced to a source:** *loko*, *mwɔnɛ*, *ekohu*, *blɛuu*, *kulaa*, *saminya*, *jã nɔuu*, *nimeli*, *akɛnɛ*, *agbo* (door), *he hia*, *faa*, *Vɛshɔn*, *klashi*.
6. **"At once":** *amlɔ nɛ ɔ* is used throughout, though JW.ORG was seen using it for "now". Is there a better word?
7. **Minutes:** only the abbreviation *Hɛngm.* was found, so it is used everywhere, including *hɛngm. 60* for "one hour". What is the full word? Would it read better spelled out, and is a word for "hour" better in the page's opening line?
8. **Left and right:** left in English (*left je*, *(right)*). What are the right words?
9. **Slider:** *Gbla kpɔ ɔ kɛ ya hɛ mi (right)*. In the help page, does *kɛ ya hɛ mi* make the text bigger?

**B. Taken from the reviewed Ga translation and adapted (does the Dangme match?)**
10. **Weekend meeting:** *Otsi Nyagbe Kpe* (Ga *Otsi Naagbee Kpee*). Not found on JW.ORG in Dangme. What do congregations say? Also *Hwɔɔmi Mɔ Ní Kasemi* for "Watchtower Study".
11. **Exercise and leader labels:** *Ní Peemi 1.1*; *Klas ɔ Hɛ Nyɛɛlɔ* (facilitator); *Nɔ́ Nɛ Tsɔɔlɔ ɔ Maa Tsɔɔ* (Instructor Demonstration); *nihi nɛ yeɔ bua* (helpers); *ní kaseli* (students).
12. **Main navigation bar:** *kpɔ nɛ fãhi ɔmɛ ngɛ nɔ ɔ*.
13. **Loanwords (spelling and naturalness):** *mobail data*, *batri*, *chaaja*, *skrin* (or *skrini*?), *kiibɔd*, *kamera*, *skani*, *printi*, *kliki*, *maus*, *sisitɛm*, *sɛkɛnd*, *fail*, *backup*, *Wi-Fi okadi*, *speaker okadi*, *QR okadi*.
14. **Colours:** *yɛlo*, *bluu*, *grin*. Are there Dangme words?
15. **Dark and Light buttons:** *Dun* and *La*. In Dangme *La* also means "song". Is another word better?
16. **Table:** *okplɔ*. Spelling?
17. **Arrive early and welcome:** *Ba Ngɛ Be Mi*, and *Mo Hee* at the start of class.
18. **Sun icon:** *hulu okadi* (Class Program PDF, Exercise 1.1).
19. **Added to Ga after its review, so not yet checked in Ga either:** the language-help sentence, *Ke ní kaselɔ ko ngɛ womihi hlae ngɛ gbi kpa ko mi ɔ, moo nyɛ **Languages** okadi ɔ (A/あ) (Gbi) nɔ, nɛ o hla gbi ɔ.* and the Mac row in the README, *macOS 13 loo nɔ́ nɛ he hi pe jã, nɛ Apple Silicon (M1 chip loo nɔ́ nɛ he hi pe jã) ngɛ mi*.

**C. Found on JW.ORG, but please confirm the use**
20. **Video title:** *Mɛni He Je Nɛ E Sa Kaa Waa Kase Baiblo ɔ?* From *lff* Lesson 1, where it continues *—Nɔ́ Nɛ E Nya Kɛ*. Is this *Why Study the Bible?*
21. **Page title:** *Ngɔɔ JW Library ɔ Kɛ Tsu Ní* (from the help) and the subtitle *Ní Kasemi Womi Kɛ Klas ɔ He Blɔ Nya Tomi*. Note that *Ní Kasemi Womi* is also the workbook's short name. Could this confuse learners?
22. **Tips:** *Ga Womi* for "tip". In a guide used alongside the Ga tutorial, could "Ga" be misread as the language name? Is another word better?
23. **Top of the screen:** *yiti je*. Bottom: *sisi*.
24. **Device words:** *klama* (device), *fon*, *tablɛti*, *kɔmpiuta*.
25. **The label *KAI:*** ("NOTE:") at the top of `JWLibrary_Class.md` and both PDFs is the translator's choice. *Nɔ́ nɛ he hia:* is used for "Important:".
