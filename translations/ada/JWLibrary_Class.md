## Ní Peemihi Kɛ Ha Ní Kaseli

### 1. Ní Peemihi: Bɔ Nɛ O Maa Na Ngmami Ɔmɛ Kɛ Asafo Mi Kpehi A Níhi Ha

* **Ní Peemi 1.1. Daa Ligbi Ngmami ɔ Kɛ Ningma Amɛ A Klemi:** Moo nyɛ **Daily Text** (☀️) *(Daa Ligbi Ngmami)* nɔ. Ngɛ yiti je ɔ, moo nyɛ **Text Settings** (**`A A`**) *(Ningma amɛ a klemi)* okadi ɔ nɔ, nɛ o gbla kpɔ ɔ kɛ ya hɛ mi (right) konɛ ningma amɛ nɛ hi agbo nɛ o nyɛ nɛ o kane.
* **Ní Peemi 1.2. Baiblo Yi:** Moo nyɛ **Bible** (📖) *(Baiblo)* nɔ. Hla Baiblo womi ko (kaa nɔ́ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, *Mateo*) kɛ yi ko (kaa nɔ́ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, *5*) nɛ o kane.
* **Ní Peemi 1.3. Meetings:** Moo nyɛ **Meetings** (📅) *(Asafo Mi Kpehi)* nɔ. Bli otsi nɛ ɔ *Hwɔɔmi Mɔ* Ní Kasemi munyu ɔ kɛ je Otsi Nyagbe Kpe (Weekend Meeting) ɔ he blɔ nya tomi ɔ mi amlɔ nɛ ɔ.
* *Tsɔɔlɔ ɔ maa tsɔɔ (kɛ piɛɛ he):* Ngɔɔ **Bookmark** okadi ɔ (🔖) kɛ kadi ngmami ko, ngɔɔ **History** okadi ɔ (🕒) kɛ na ngmamihi kɛ munyuhi nɛ o kane hyɛ ɔ, loo ngɔɔ **Search** okadi ɔ (🔍) kɛ hla munyu ko.

### 2. Ní Peemihi: Níhi Nɛ A Gblaa Kɛ Bɔ Nɛ A Kɛ Tsuɔ Ní Ha

* **Ní Peemi 2.1. Wi-Fi ɔ Hyɛmi:** Loko o maa gbla video loo munyuhi nɛ a buɔ tue ɔ, hyɛ kaa o fon, o tablɛti, loo o kɔmpiuta a ngɛ Wi-Fi ɔ nɔ lo, konɛ o mobail data a ko dá.
* **Ní Peemi 2.2. Gblami Konɛ O Kɛ Tsu Ní Ke Intanɛti Be:** Moo nyɛ **Publications** (📚) *(Womihi)* loo asafo mi kpehi a he blɔ nya tomi ɔ nɔ. Moo nyɛ **okadi nɛ ngɛ kaa bɔku ɔ (☁️)** nɔ ngɛ womi loo video nyafii ko he konɛ o gbla nɛ o kɛ tsu ní ke intanɛti be.
* **Ní Peemi 2.3. Bu Tue, Nɛ O Ha Gbi ɔ Nɛ Kpa:** Bli Baiblo yi ko. Moo nyɛ **Headphones / Play (🎧)** okadi nɛ ngɛ skrin ɔ yiti je ɔ nɔ konɛ o bu tue. Moo kase bɔ nɛ o maa ha gbi ɔ nɛ kpa ha amlɔ nɛ ɔ: ngɛ fon loo tablɛti nɔ ɔ, moo nyɛ **Volume Down** nɛ ngɛ he ɔ nɔ nɛ o kɛ o nine nɛ pi nɔ; ngɛ kɔmpiuta nɔ ɔ, moo nyɛ **Mute (🔇)** ngɛ o kiibɔd ɔ nɔ; loo moo nyɛ/moo kliki **Pause (⏸️)**.
* *Tsɔɔlɔ ɔ maa tsɔɔ (kɛ piɛɛ he):* Ngɛ **Publications** > **Downloaded** mi ɔ, to níhi ɔmɛ a nya kɛ **Largest Size** konɛ o hyɛ blɔ nɛ níhi ɔmɛ ngɔ, nɛ o na bɔ nɛ o maa je níhi nɛ a he hia mo hu ɔ ha.

### 3. Ní Peemihi: Níhi Nɛ O Ma Nyɛ Maa Kɛ Tsu Ní Ngɛ Dɛ He Ní Kasemi Mi

* **Ní Peemi 3.1. Ngɔɔ Kɔla Kɛ Kadi Heto Ko:** Ngɛ otsi nɛ ɔ *Hwɔɔmi Mɔ* Ní Kasemi munyu ɔ mi ɔ, o kɛ o nine nɛ pi heto nɛ ngɛ kuku 1 ɔ mi ɔ munyungu klɛdɛɛ ɔ nɔ, nɛ o ngɔ o nine kɛ pi munyu ɔ nɔ bɔɔ, nɛ o hla **yɛlo**.
* **Ní Peemi 3.2. Tsake Kɔla a:** Moo nyɛ munyu nɛ o kɛ kɔla kadi ɔ nɔ ekohu. Hla kɔla kpa ko (kaa bluu loo grin), loo moo nyɛ **Delete** okadi ɔ nɔ konɛ o je kɔla a.
* **Ní Peemi 3.3. Moo Pee Backup:** Moo nyɛ **Settings** (⚙️) > **Backup and Restore** > **Create Backup** nɔ. Ngɔɔ o backup ɔ (`.jwlibrary`) kɛ to konɛ níhi nɛ o kadi ngɛ o ní kasemi mi ɔ ko laa.
* *Tsɔɔlɔ ɔ maa tsɔɔ (kɛ piɛɛ he):* Moo nyɛ munyu nɛ o kɛ kɔla kadi ɔ nɔ, hla **Add Note** (📝), nɛ o ya **Personal Study** *(Dɛ He Ní Kasemi)* fã ɔ nɔ konɛ o na níhi tsuo nɛ o ngma, o bookmark ɔmɛ, kɛ níhi nɛ o kɛ kɔla kadi ɔ ngɛ he kake.
