## 📋 Nikasemɔi Lɛ Ahe Saji Yɛ Kuku Mli

Nikasemɔi etɛ yɛ mli, ni eko fɛɛ eko yeɔ miniti 15. Yelikɛbuamɔ ni afeɔ miniti 15 dani klas lɛ bɔi, shishijee ni yeɔ miniti 5, kɛ naagbee ni yeɔ miniti 10 hu yɛ mli:

0. **Yelikɛbuamɔ Dani Klas Lɛ Bɔi Kɛ Shishijee (Miniti 5–15)**
   * **Yelikɛbuamɔ Kɛhã Mɛi Ni Ba Mra (miniti 15 dani klas lɛ bɔi):** Yelikɛbualɔi lɛ hereɔ mɛi ni ba lɛ, amɛyeɔ amɛbuaa amɛ ni amɛyaa Wi-Fi ni yɔɔ he ni afeɔ klas lɛ yɛ lɛ nɔ, ni amɛkwɛɔ akɛ Biblia lɛ kɛ Daa Gbi Ŋmalɛ Lɛ edaunlodi momo lo.
   * **Akwɛ Yɛ Sekɛnd 30 Mli:** Nikaselɔi lɛ woɔ amɛnine nɔ kɛ́ amɛmiitao yelikɛbuamɔ koni amɛdaunlodi Biblia lɛ, ni yelikɛbualɔi lɛ yeɔ amɛbuaa amɛ be ni klas lɛ eebɔi lɛ.
   * **Toiŋjɔlɛ (Bɔ Ni Akɛhãa Gbee Sɛɛ Foɔ):** Atsɔɔ nikaselɔi lɛ bɔ ni amɛaafee amɛhã gbee ni amɛkpaaa gbɛ lɛ sɛɛ afo oya nɔŋŋ: **Volume Down** ni yɔɔ foŋ/tablɛt lɛ he, **Mute (🔇)** yɛ kɔmpiuta okiibɔd lɛ nɔ, loo **Pause (⏸️)** yɛ skrin lɛ nɔ.
   * Akwɛ he ni kpɔ ni fãi titrii lɛ yɔɔ nɔ lɛ (navigation bar) yɔɔ, ni atsake be ni skrin lɛ gbeɔ lɛ.

1. **Nikasemɔ 1: Bɔ Ni Obaafee Ona Ŋmalɛi Kɛ Asafoŋ Kpeei Anibii (Miniti 15)**
   * Kpɔ ni fãi titrii lɛ yɔɔ nɔ lɛ, kɛ ŋmɛnɛ **Daily Text** *(Daa Gbi Ŋmalɛ Lɛ)* kanemɔ.
   * **Niŋmai lɛ adaramɔ (`A A`)** tsakemɔ koni akane mlɛo.
   * **Biblia wolo kɛ yitso** pɔtɛɛ ko gbelemɔ.
   * **Meetings** *(Asafoŋ Kpeei)* fã lɛ: oya *Wɔshiɛmɔ Kɛ Wɔshihilɛ Kpee Nifeemɔ Wolo* kɛhã Otsi Teŋ Kpee lɛ, kɛ *Buu-Mɔɔ* Nikasemɔ sane kɛhã Otsi Naagbee Kpee lɛ nɔ oya nɔŋŋ.
   * *Tsɔɔlɔ lɛ baatsɔɔ:* Bookmark, History, kɛ Search.

2. **Nikasemɔ 2: Nibii Ni Adaunlodiɔ Kɛ Bɔ Ni Akɛtsuɔ Nii (Miniti 15)**
   * Wi-Fi nɔ yaa dani adaunlodi nibii, koni akafite mobail data.
   * Woji, vidioi, kɛ nibii ni atswaa aboɔ toi daunlodimɔ koni akɛtsu nii kɛ́ Intanɛt bɛ (kɛ́ odaunlodi ogbe naa lɛ, ehe ehiaaa Intanɛt yɛ asafoŋ kpeei ashishi).
   * **Nibii ni atswaa aboɔ toi (🎧)**: Biblia yitsei, Daa Gbi Ŋmalɛ Lɛ, loo saji ni akaseɔ ni akaneɔ yɛ gbee mli lɛ toi bomɔ.
   * **Bɔ Ni Akɛhãa Gbee Sɛɛ Foɔ Oya Nɔŋŋ:** Volume Down ni yɔɔ foŋ lɛ he (nyɛmɔ nɔ ni ashi nine yɛ nɔ), Mute yɛ kɔmpiuta okiibɔd lɛ nɔ, loo Pause yɛ skrin lɛ nɔ.
   * *Tsɔɔlɔ lɛ baatsɔɔ:* Gbɛ ni yɔɔ foŋ lɛ nɔ kwɛmɔ, kɛ nibii toomɔ yɛ **Largest Size** *(Edalɛ)* naa koni ajie nibii ni ehe ehiaaa.

3. **Nikasemɔ 3: Nibii Ni Obaanyɛ Okɛtsu Nii Yɛ Aŋkro Nikasemɔ Mli (Miniti 15)**
   * Yɛlo kɔla ni akɛkadiɔ hetoi yɛ saji ni akaseɔ kɛ Biblia kukuji amli.
   * Kɔla lɛ tsakemɔ (akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, kɛya bluu loo grin), loo kɔla lɛ jiemɔ.
   * **Bɔ Ni Abuɔ Nibii Ahe:** Backup fail (`.jwlibrary`) feemɔ yɛ *Settings > Backup and Restore* mli, koni nibii ni oŋmala kɛ nibii ni okɛ kɔla kadi lɛ akalaaje.
   * *Tsɔɔlɔ lɛ baatsɔɔ:* Nibii ni aŋmalaa afatamɔ he, **Tags**, kɛ **Personal Study** *(Aŋkro Nikasemɔ)* fã lɛ.

4. **Naagbee: Nɔ Ni Wɔkase Lɛ Akwɛ Ekoŋŋ, Kɛ Sanebimɔi (Miniti 10)**
   * Miniti 10 kɛhã nɔ ni akase lɛ kwɛmɔ ekoŋŋ, sanebimɔi, loo be ni he baahia kɛ́ nifeemɔ ko ye be fe bɔ ni akpa gbɛ.
   * Klas lɛ hiɛnyiɛlɔi baanyɛ ahi jɛmɛ yɛ klas lɛ sɛɛ koni amɛye amɛbua mɔ fɛɛ mɔ ni miitao yelikɛbuamɔ pɔtɛɛ.
   * *Ŋaawoo kɛhã klas lɛ hiɛnyiɛlɔ:* Kɛ́ Intanɛt fa loo ewa lɛ, kɛ Klas Lɛ He Gbɛjianɔtoo ni yɔɔ baafa kome nɔ ni aprinti lɛ afɔ̃ okpɔlɔi lɛ anɔ; okɛ QR wolo bibii afɔ̃ okpɔlɔi lɛ anɔ koni nikaselɔi akɛ amɛfoŋ kamera agbele kasemɔ wolo lɛ; ni otsɔɔ amɛ "Add to Home Screen" koni kasemɔ wolo lɛ agbele kɛ́ Intanɛt bɛ po.

## 📱 Foŋ, Tablɛt, Kɛ Kɔmpiuta Ni Abaanyɛ Akɛtsu Nii

Koni onine ashɛ nibii hei kɛ bɔ ni abuɔ app lɛ he lɛ nɔ lɛ, kwɛmɔ akɛ ofoŋ, otablɛt, loo okɔmpiuta lɛ kɛ nɔ ni app lɛ miitao lɛ kpãa gbee lo:

| Sisitɛm | Vɛɛshiŋ Ni Esa Akɛ Oná |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Android** | Android 8.0 loo nɔ ni yɛ hiɛ fe nakai |
| **Apple iOS / iPadOS** | iOS 15.0 loo nɔ ni yɛ hiɛ fe nakai |
| **Mac** | macOS ni Apple Silicon (M1 chip loo nɔ ni yɛ hiɛ fe nakai) yɔɔ mli |
| **Windows** | Windows 10 Version 2004 loo nɔ ni yɛ hiɛ fe nakai |

### Nibii Ni Esa Akɛ Ofee Dani Oba:
* **Ba Mra (Miniti 10–15 Dani Klas Lɛ Bɔi):** Ba mra koni yelikɛbualɔi lɛ aye abua bo ni oya Wi-Fi lɛ nɔ, ni amɛkwɛ akɛ Biblia lɛ kɛ Daa Gbi Ŋmalɛ Lɛ yɛ ofoŋ lɛ nɔ lo.
* **Batri Lɛ Ayi Obɔ:** Kwɛmɔ ni ofoŋ, otablɛt, loo okɔmpiuta lɛ batri eyi obɔ dani oba (loo okɛ ochaaja aba).
* **Be Ni Skrin Lɛ Gbeɔ:** Tsakemɔ be ni oskrin lɛ gbeɔ (screen timeout / auto-lock) lɛ kɛya miniti 2 loo 5 yɛ klas lɛ shishi, koni skrin lɛ akagbe be ni atsɔɔ nii lɛ.
* **App Lɛ Ayɛ Nɔ:** Daunlodimɔ *JW Library* app lɛ kɛjɛ app store ni sa lɛ nɔ owo ofoŋ, otablɛt, loo okɔmpiuta lɛ nɔ dani klas lɛ bɔi.
* **Gbɛ Yɛ Ofoŋ Lɛ Nɔ:** Kwɛmɔ akɛ gbɛ ni shɛɔ **GB 2 kɛyashi 4** yɛ ofoŋ lɛ nɔ (loo SD card), koni onyɛ odaunlodi woji, nibii ni atswaa aboɔ toi, kɛ vidioi mlɛo.
* **Wi-Fi:** Yaa Wi-Fi nɔ dani odaunlodi vidioi kɛ nibii ni atswaa aboɔ toi ni da, koni okafite omobail data.
* **Bɔ Ni Akɛhãa Gbee Sɛɛ Foɔ:** Kaimɔ akɛ kɛ́ onyɛ **Volume Down** nɔ (foŋ/tablɛt) loo **Mute (🔇)** yɛ okiibɔd lɛ nɔ (kɔmpiuta), gbee lɛ sɛɛ baafo oya nɔŋŋ.
