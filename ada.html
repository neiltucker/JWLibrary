📖 Ní Kasemi 1: Bɔ Nɛ O Maa Na Ngmami Ɔmɛ Kɛ Asafo Mi Kpehi A Níhi Ha

Be: Hɛngm. 15

Moo kase bɔ nɛ o maa ya app ɔ fãhi ɔmɛ a nɔ ha, bɔ nɛ o maa ha ningma amɛ nɛ hi agbo konɛ o nyɛ nɛ o kane ha, bɔ nɛ o maa bli Baiblo ɔ yi tsuaa yi ha, kɛ bɔ nɛ o maa ya níhi nɛ o maa kɛ dla o he kɛ ha asafo mi kpehi daa otsi ɔ a nɔ ha.

▶️ Hyɛ Blɔ Tsɔɔmi Nɛ Ngɛ JW.ORG Nɔ (Ngɛ Blɔfo Mi)

Ní Peemi 1.1: Daa Ligbi Ngmami ɔ Kɛ Bɔ Nɛ O Maa Ha Ningma Amɛ Nɛ Hi Agbo Ha

  • 1 Hyɛ navigation bar ɔ (ngɛ o skrin ɔ sisi), nɛ o nyɛ Daily Text (☀️) (Daa Ligbi Ngmami) okadi ɔ nɔ.
  • 2 Hyɛ o skrin ɔ yiti je ɔ, nɛ o nyɛ Text Settings / Appearance okadi ɔ (A A) (Ningma amɛ a klemi) nɔ.
  • 3 Gbla kpɔ ɔ kɛ ya hɛ mi (right) konɛ ningma amɛ nɛ hi agbo, nɛ o nyɛ nɛ o kane.
💡 Ga Womi Kɛ Ha Kanemi: Ke o hla ningma amɛ a klemi nɛ o suɔ ngɛ biɛ ɔ, jã nɔuu e maa ngɛ ngɛ Baiblo yi ɔmɛ kɛ munyuhi tsuo a mi.

Ní Peemi 1.2: Bli Baiblo Womi Ko Kɛ Yi Ko

  • 1 Moo nyɛ Bible (📖) (Baiblo) okadi ɔ nɔ ngɛ navigation bar ɔ nɔ.
  • 2 Moo nyɛ Baiblo womi ɔmɛ a eko nɔ (kaa nɔ́ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Mateo).
  • 3 Moo nyɛ yi 5 nɔ konɛ o kane.

Ní Peemi 1.3: Ngɔɔ Meetings Fã ɔ Kɛ Tsu Ní

  • 1 Moo nyɛ Meetings (📅) (Asafo Mi Kpehi) okadi ɔ nɔ ngɛ navigation bar ɔ nɔ.
  • 2 Hyɛ asafo mi kpehi enyɔ nɛ a peeɔ daa otsi ɔ: Si Himi Kɛ Sɔmɔmi Ní Tsumi Kpe (Midweek Meeting) kɛ Otsi Nyagbe Kpe (Weekend Meeting).
  • 3 Ngɛ Otsi Nyagbe Kpe ɔ sisi ɔ, moo nyɛ Hwɔɔmi Mɔ Ní Kasemi (Watchtower Study) munyu ɔ nɔ konɛ e bli amlɔ nɛ ɔ.

Nɔ́ Nɛ Tsɔɔlɔ ɔ Maa Tsɔɔ: Bookmark, History, Kɛ Search

Tsɔɔlɔ ɔ maa tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ a kɛ Bookmark (🔖) kadiɔ he nɛ o su ɔ ha, bɔ nɛ a kɛ History (🕒) naa ngmamihi kɛ munyuhi nɛ o kane hyɛ ɔ ha, kɛ bɔ nɛ a kɛ Search (🔍) hlaa munyu ko ha. O ma nyɛ maa kase nɛ ɔmɛ ngɛ we!

⬇️ Ní Kasemi 2: Níhi Nɛ A Gblaa Kɛ Bɔ Nɛ A Kɛ Tsuɔ Ní Ha

Be: Hɛngm. 15

Moo kase bɔ nɛ o maa kane níhi ha ke o be intanɛti ɔ nɔ po ɔ, bɔ nɛ o maa gbla womihi ha be nɛ o ngɛ Wi-Fi ɔ nɔ konɛ o mobail data a ko dá, kɛ bɔ nɛ o maa bu munyuhi nɛ a fiaa nɛ a buɔ tue ɔ tue ha.

▶️ Hyɛ Blɔ Tsɔɔmi Nɛ Ngɛ JW.ORG Nɔ (Ngɛ Blɔfo Mi)

Ní Peemi 2.1: Hyɛ Wi-Fi ɔ, Konɛ O Mobail Data a Ko Puɛ

  • 1 Hyɛ o skrin ɔ yiti je ɔ konɛ o na kaa Wi-Fi okadi ɔ ngɛ jamɛ a he, nɛ o ngɛ Wi-Fi ɔ nɔ.
  • 2 Mɛni he je nɛ nɛ ɔ he hia: Ke o gbla níhi be nɛ o ngɛ Wi-Fi ɔ nɔ ɔ, o mobail data a puɛ we.
  • 3 Tue mi jɔmi ke o be intanɛti ɔ nɔ: Ke o gbla womihi kɛ munyuhi nɛ a buɔ tue ɔ, a hiɔ o klama a nɔ; e he hia we kaa o ya intanɛti ɔ nɔ loko o kɛ tsu ní ngɛ asafo mi kpehi a sisi!

Ní Peemi 2.2: Gbla Nɔ́ Ko Konɛ O Kɛ Tsu Ní Ke O Be Intanɛti ɔ Nɔ Po

  • 1 Moo nyɛ Publications (📚) (Womihi) okadi ɔ nɔ, loo moo ya Meetings (📅) (Asafo Mi Kpehi) fã ɔ nɔ.
  • 2 Hla nɔ́ ko nɛ okadi nɛ ngɛ kaa bɔku ɔ (☁️) ngɛ he (kaa nɔ́ hyɛmi nɔ́ ɔ, Ní Kasemi Womi nɛ ngɛ amlɔ nɛ ɔ, loo video nyafii ko).
  • 3 Moo nyɛ nɔ́ ɔ nɔ konɛ o gbla. Ke bɔku okadi ɔ je jamɛ a he ɔ, e tsɔɔ kaa e ngɛ o klama a nɔ, nɛ o ma nyɛ maa kane ke o be intanɛti ɔ nɔ po ɔ.

Ní Peemi 2.3: Bu Tue, Nɛ O Kase Bɔ Nɛ O Maa Ha Gbi ɔ Nɛ Kpa Amlɔ Nɛ ɔ Ha

  • 1 Bli Baiblo yi ko.
  • 2 Moo nyɛ Headphones / Play okadi ɔ (🎧) nɛ ngɛ o skrin ɔ yiti je ɔ nɔ konɛ o bu nɔ́ nɛ a ngɛ kanee ɔ tue.
  • 3 Moo kase bɔ nɛ o maa ha gbi ɔ nɛ kpa ha: Ha gbi ɔ nɛ kpa amlɔ nɛ ɔ: moo nyɛ Pause (⏸️) nɛ ngɛ o skrin ɔ nɔ ɔ nɔ, loo moo nyɛ Volume Down nɛ ngɛ o fon ɔ he ɔ nɔ (loo Mute ngɛ kɔmpiuta kiibɔd ɔ nɔ).
🔇 Ga Womi Kɛ Ha Tue Mi Jɔmi: Ko ye gbeye kaa gbi ko maa je kpo be nɛ o hyɛ we blɔ. Ngɛ fon loo tablɛti nɔ ɔ, ke o nyɛ Volume Down nɛ ngɛ he ɔ nɔ nɛ o kɛ o nine nɛ pi nɔ ɔ, gbi ɔ maa kpa. Ngɛ kɔmpiuta nɔ ɔ, moo nyɛ Mute (🔇) ngɛ o kiibɔd ɔ nɔ, loo moo kliki Pause (⏸️).

Nɔ́ Nɛ Tsɔɔlɔ ɔ Maa Tsɔɔ: Bɔ Nɛ O Maa Ná Blɔ Ngɛ O Klama a Nɔ Ha

Tsɔɔlɔ ɔ maa tsɔɔ bɔ nɛ o maa ya Publications > Downloaded ha, nɛ o to níhi ɔmɛ a nya kɛ Largest Size, nɛ o je videohi nɛ a he hia mo hu ɔ ke blɔ nɛ ngɛ o klama a nɔ ɔ ngɛ bee ɔ.

🖍️ Ní Kasemi 3: Níhi Nɛ O Ma Nyɛ Maa Kɛ Tsu Ní Ngɛ Dɛ He Ní Kasemi Mi

Moo kase bɔ nɛ o maa ngɔ kɔla kɛ kadi hetohi kɛ ha asafo mi kpe ha, bɔ nɛ o maa tsake kɔla a loo o je ha, kɛ bɔ nɛ o maa pee backup ha konɛ níhi nɛ o kadi ngɛ dɛ he ní kasemi mi jehahi babauu a ko laa.

▶️ Hyɛ Blɔ Tsɔɔmi Nɛ Ngɛ JW.ORG Nɔ (Ngɛ Blɔfo Mi)

Ní Peemi 3.1: Ngɔɔ Yɛlo Kɔla Kɛ Kadi Heto Ko

  • 1 Moo nyɛ Meetings (📅) (Asafo Mi Kpehi) okadi ɔ nɔ, nɛ o bli Hwɔɔmi Mɔ munyu nɛ a maa kase ngɛ Hwɔɔmi Mɔ Ní Kasemi nɛ maa ba ɔ sisi ɔ.
  • 2 Ngɛ kuku 1 ɔ mi ɔ, o kɛ o nine nɛ pi heto ɔ mi munyungu klɛdɛɛ ɔ nɔ, nɛ o ngɔ o nine kɛ pi munyu ɔ nɔ bɔɔ.
  • 3 Moo nyɛ yɛlo kɔla a nɔ ngɛ kɔlahi nɛ maa je kpo ɔ a kpɛti. A kɛ kɔla kadi o heto ɔ!

Ní Peemi 3.2: Tsake Kɔla a, Loo O Je

  • 1 Moo nyɛ munyu nɛ o kɛ yɛlo kɔla kadi amlɔ nɛ ɔ nɔ.
  • 2 Moo nyɛ kɔla kpa ko (kaa bluu loo grin) nɔ konɛ o tsake.
  • 3 Ke o suɔ nɛ o je kɔla a kulaa a, moo nyɛ Delete okadi ɔ (🗑️) nɔ.

Ní Peemi 3.3: Moo Pee Backup Konɛ O Níhi Ko Laa

  • 1 Moo nyɛ Settings (⚙️) nɔ ngɛ o skrin ɔ nɔ.
  • 2 Moo nyɛ Backup and Restore nɔ.
  • 3 Moo nyɛ Create Backup nɔ.
  • 4 Moo ya nɔ kɛ blɔ tsɔɔmihi nɛ maa je kpo ngɛ o skrin ɔ nɔ ɔ konɛ o ngɔ o backup ɔ (.jwlibrary) kɛ to.
🛡️ Ga Womi Kɛ Ha Tue Mi Jɔmi: Níhi nɛ o ngma kɛ níhi nɛ o kɛ kɔla kadi ɔ ngɛ o klama a nɔ. Ke o peeɔ backup daa ɔ, ke o ná fon loo tablɛti hee po ɔ, o ma nyɛ maa ná o níhi ɔmɛ tsuo ekohu.
