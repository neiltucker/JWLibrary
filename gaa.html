📖 Nikasemɔ 1: Bɔ Ni Obaafee Ona Ŋmalɛi Kɛ Asafoŋ Kpeei Anibii

Be: Miniti 15

Kasemɔ bɔ ni obaafee oya app lɛ fãi titrii lɛ anɔ, ohã niŋmai lɛ adara koni onyɛ okane mlɛo, ogbele Biblia lɛ yitso fɛɛ yitso, ni oya nibii ni okɛbaasaa ohe kɛhã asafoŋ kpeei daa otsi lɛ anɔ oya nɔŋŋ.

▶️ Kwɛmɔ Gbɛtsɔɔmɔ Ni Yɔɔ JW.ORG Nɔ (yɛ Blɔfo mli)

Nifeemɔ 1.1: Daa Gbi Ŋmalɛ Lɛ Kɛ Bɔ Ni Obaafee Ohã Niŋmai Lɛ Adara

  • 1 Kwɛmɔ kpɔ ni fãi titrii lɛ yɔɔ nɔ lɛ (yɛ oskrin lɛ shishigbɛ), ni onyɛ Daily Text (☀️) (Daa Gbi Ŋmalɛ Lɛ) lɛ nɔ.
  • 2 Kwɛmɔ oskrin lɛ ŋwɛigbɛ, ni onyɛ Text Settings / Appearance okadi lɛ (A A) (Niŋmai lɛ adaramɔ) nɔ.
  • 3 Tsi kpɔ lɛ kɛya hiɛ (ninejurɔgbɛ) koni niŋmai lɛ adara, ni onyɛ okane mlɛo.
💡 Ŋaawoo Kɛhã Nikanemɔ: Kɛ́ ohala niŋmai lɛ adaramɔ ni osumɔɔ yɛ biɛ lɛ, nakai ebaapue yɛ Biblia yitsei kɛ saji fɛɛ amli.

Nifeemɔ 1.2: Gbelemɔ Biblia Wolo Ko Kɛ Yitso Ko

  • 1 Nyɛɛ Bible (📖) (Biblia) lɛ nɔ yɛ kpɔ ni fãi titrii lɛ yɔɔ nɔ lɛ nɔ.
  • 2 Nyɛɛ Biblia woji lɛ eko nɔ (akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, Mateo).
  • 3 Nyɛɛ yitso 5 nɔ koni okane.

Nifeemɔ 1.3: Okɛ Meetings Fã Lɛ Atsu Nii

  • 1 Nyɛɛ Meetings (📅) (Asafoŋ Kpeei) lɛ nɔ yɛ kpɔ ni fãi titrii lɛ yɔɔ nɔ lɛ nɔ.
  • 2 Kwɛmɔ asafoŋ kpeei enyɔ ni afeɔ daa otsi lɛ: Otsi Teŋ Kpee (Midweek Meeting) kɛ Otsi Naagbee Kpee (Weekend Meeting).
  • 3 Yɛ Otsi Naagbee Kpee lɛ shishi lɛ, nyɛɛ Buu-Mɔɔ Nikasemɔ (Watchtower Study) sane lɛ nɔ koni egbele ehã bo oya nɔŋŋ.

Nɔkwɛmɔnɔ Ni Tsɔɔlɔ Lɛ Baafee: Bookmark, History, Kɛ Search

Tsɔɔlɔ lɛ baatsɔɔ bɔ ni akɛ Bookmark (🔖) kadiɔ he ni oshɛ, bɔ ni akɛ History (🕒) naa ŋmalɛi ni okane etsɛko tsɔ lɛ, kɛ bɔ ni akɛ Search (🔍) taoɔ wiemɔ ko. Obaanyɛ okase enɛɛmɛi yɛ shĩa!

⬇️ Nikasemɔ 2: Nibii Ni Adaunlodiɔ Kɛ Bɔ Ni Akɛtsuɔ Nii

Be: Miniti 15

Kasemɔ bɔ ni obaafee okane nibii kɛ́ obɛ Intanɛt lɛ nɔ po, odaunlodi woji be ni oyɛ Wi-Fi lɛ nɔ koni okafite omobail data, ni obo nibii ni atswaa aboɔ toi lɛ toi.

▶️ Kwɛmɔ Gbɛtsɔɔmɔ Ni Yɔɔ JW.ORG Nɔ (yɛ Blɔfo mli)

Nifeemɔ 2.1: Kwɛmɔ Wi-Fi Lɛ, Ni Okafite Omobail Data

  • 1 Kwɛmɔ oskrin lɛ ŋwɛigbɛ koni ona akɛ Wi-Fi okadi lɛ yɛ jɛmɛ, ni oyɛ Wi-Fi lɛ nɔ.
  • 2 Mɛni hewɔ enɛ he hiaa: Kɛ́ odaunlodi nibii be ni oyɛ Wi-Fi lɛ nɔ lɛ, ofiteŋ omobail data.
  • 3 Toiŋjɔlɛ kɛ́ Intanɛt bɛ: Kɛ́ odaunlodi woji kɛ nibii ni atswaa aboɔ toi ogbe naa lɛ, amɛhiɔ ofoŋ lɛ nɔ; ehe ehiaaa ni oya Intanɛt lɛ nɔ dani onyɛ okɛtsu nii yɛ asafoŋ kpeei ashishi!

Nifeemɔ 2.2: Daunlodimɔ Nɔ Ko Koni Okɛtsu Nii Kɛ́ Intanɛt Bɛ

  • 1 Nyɛɛ Publications (📚) (Woji kɛ Nibii) lɛ nɔ, loo oya Meetings (📅) (Asafoŋ Kpeei) fã lɛ nɔ.
  • 2 Taomɔ nɔ ko ni atatu okadi (☁️) yɔɔ he (akɛ nɔkwɛmɔnɔ lɛ, Wɔshiɛmɔ Kɛ Wɔshihilɛ Kpee Nifeemɔ Wolo ni yɔɔ nɔ amrɔ nɛɛ lɛ, loo vidio bibioo ko).
  • 3 Nyɛɛ nɔ lɛ nɔ koni odaunlodi. Kɛ́ atatu okadi lɛ laaje lɛ, no tsɔɔ akɛ ekã ofoŋ lɛ nɔ, ni obaanyɛ okane kɛ́ Intanɛt bɛ po.

Nifeemɔ 2.3: Bo Toi, Ni Okase Bɔ Ni Obaafee Ohã Gbee Lɛ Sɛɛ Afo Oya Nɔŋŋ

  • 1 Gbelemɔ ŋmɛnɛ Daa Gbi Ŋmalɛ Lɛ, loo Biblia yitso ko.
  • 2 Nyɛɛ Headphones / Play okadi lɛ (🎧) ni yɔɔ oskrin lɛ ŋwɛigbɛ lɛ nɔ koni obo nɔ ni akaneɔ lɛ toi.
  • 3 Kasemɔ bɔ ni obaafee ohã gbee lɛ sɛɛ afo: Hã gbee lɛ sɛɛ afo oya nɔŋŋ: nyɛɛ Pause (⏸️) ni yɔɔ oskrin lɛ nɔ lɛ nɔ, loo onyɛ Volume Down ni yɔɔ ofoŋ lɛ he lɛ nɔ (loo Mute yɛ kɔmpiuta okiibɔd lɛ nɔ).
🔇 Ŋaawoo Kɛhã Toiŋjɔlɛ: Okashe gbeyei akɛ gbee ko baaje kpo be ni okpaaa gbɛ. Yɛ foŋ loo tablɛt nɔ lɛ, kɛ́ onyɛ Volume Down ni yɔɔ he lɛ nɔ ni oshi onine yɛ nɔ lɛ, gbee lɛ sɛɛ baafo. Yɛ kɔmpiuta nɔ lɛ, nyɛɛ Mute (🔇) yɛ okiibɔd lɛ nɔ, loo okliki Pause (⏸️).

Nɔkwɛmɔnɔ Ni Tsɔɔlɔ Lɛ Baafee: Bɔ Ni Obaafee Oná Gbɛ Yɛ Ofoŋ Lɛ Nɔ

Tsɔɔlɔ lɛ baatsɔɔ bɔ ni obaafee oya Publications > Downloaded, oto nibii lɛ anaa yɛ Largest Size (Edalɛ) naa, ni ojie vidioi ni ehe ehiaaa bo dɔŋŋ lɛ kɛ́ gbɛ ni yɔɔ ofoŋ lɛ nɔ lɛ miiba shi.

🖍️ Nikasemɔ 3: Nibii Ni Obaanyɛ Okɛtsu Nii Yɛ Aŋkro Nikasemɔ Mli

Kasemɔ bɔ ni obaafee okɛ kɔla akadi hetoi kɛhã asafoŋ kpee, otsake kɔla lɛ loo ojie mlɛo, ni ofee backup koni nibii ni okɛkadi yɛ aŋkro nikasemɔ mli yɛ afii pii mli lɛ akalaaje.

▶️ Kwɛmɔ Gbɛtsɔɔmɔ Ni Yɔɔ JW.ORG Nɔ (yɛ Blɔfo mli)

Nifeemɔ 3.1: Okɛ Yɛlo Kɔla Akadi Hetoo Ko

  • 1 Nyɛɛ Meetings (📅) (Asafoŋ Kpeei) lɛ nɔ, ni ogbele Buu-Mɔɔ lɛ mli sane ni abaakase yɛ Buu-Mɔɔ Nikasemɔ ni baaba lɛ shishi.
  • 2 Yɛ kuku 1 lɛ mli lɛ, nyɛɛ hetoo lɛ mli klɛŋklɛŋ wiemɔ lɛ nɔ fioo, ni ogbala onine lɛ kɛnyiɛ sane lɛ nɔ blɛoo.
  • 3 Nyɛɛ yɛlo kɔla lɛ nɔ yɛ kɔlai ni baapue lɛ ateŋ. Akɛ kɔla ekadi ohetoo lɛ!

Nifeemɔ 3.2: Tsakemɔ Kɔla Lɛ, Loo Ojie

  • 1 Nyɛɛ wiemɔi ni okɛ yɛlo kɔla kadi amrɔ nɛɛ lɛ nɔ.
  • 2 Nyɛɛ kɔla kroko (tamɔ bluu loo grin) nɔ koni otsake.
  • 3 Kɛ́ ootao ojie kɔla lɛ kwraa lɛ, nyɛɛ Delete okadi lɛ (🗑️) nɔ.

Nifeemɔ 3.3: Feemɔ Backup Koni Onibii Akalaaje

  • 1 Nyɛɛ Settings (⚙️) lɛ nɔ yɛ oskrin lɛ nɔ.
  • 2 Nyɛɛ Backup and Restore lɛ nɔ.
  • 3 Nyɛɛ Create Backup lɛ nɔ.
  • 4 Nyiɛmɔ gbɛtsɔɔmɔi ni baapue yɛ oskrin lɛ nɔ lɛ asɛɛ koni okɛ obackup lɛ (.jwlibrary) afɔ̃ shi.
🛡️ Ŋaawoo Kɛhã Toiŋjɔlɛ: Nibii ni oŋmala kɛ nibii ni okɛ kɔla kadi lɛ yɛ ofoŋ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ nɔ. Kɛ́ ofee backup daa nyɔɔŋ lɛ, kɛ́ oná foŋ loo tablɛt hee po lɛ, obaanyɛ oná onibii lɛ fɛɛ ekoŋŋ.
