## Hands-On Student Exercises

### 1. Navigation & Meeting Exercises

* **1.1. Daily Text:** Navigate to today’s Daily Text and read the day’s scripture and comment.
* **1.2. Meetings Tab:** Tap the **Meetings** tab (📅). Open this week’s *Watchtower* Study article directly from the meeting schedule.
* **1.3. Bookmarks:** Go to a Bible chapter you frequently share. Tap the **Bookmark** icon (🔖) and save your place in an open bookmark slot.
* **1.4. History:** Tap the **History** icon (🕒) to review your recent reading list and quickly jump back to a previous scripture.
* **1.5. Search:** Tap the **Search** icon (🔍). Search for a scripture containing the phrase *"draw close to God"* or an expression of your choice.

---

### 2. Downloading & Media Exercises

* **2.1. Bible Downloads:** Tap **Bible** (📖), then tap **Languages** (🌐). Locate and download an additional Bible translation (such as the *King James Version* or an available language edition).
* **2.2. Video Download:** Open **Publications** > **Videos** (or the Media section). Locate an introductory teaching video (such as *"Why Study the Bible?"*) and download it.
* **2.3. Different Language:** Tap the **Languages** icon on the video page. Download the same video in another language (e.g., Spanish).
* **2.4. Manage Storage:** Open **Publications** > **Downloaded**. Change the sort order to **Largest Size** to identify which items take the most storage space.

---

### 3. Highlighting, Personal Study & Data Safety Exercises

* **3.1. Highlighting:** Open the article for the upcoming *Watchtower* study. Select a sentence in paragraph 1 and highlight it in yellow.
* **3.2. Adjust Highlight:** Tap the highlighted text again. Change the highlight color (e.g., to blue or green), or delete it.
* **3.3. Add a Note:** Highlight another key phrase. From the context menu, tap **Add Note** (📝) and type a brief personal comment.
* **3.4. Personal Study Hub:** Tap the **Personal Study** tab to see your collected notes, bookmarks, and highlights gathered in one place.
* **3.5. Back Up Data:** Open **Settings** (⚙️) > **Backup and Restore**. Select **Create Backup** to save a copy of all your notes and study marks to your device.
