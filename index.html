JW Library Tutorial

A practical, hands-on practice guide to help you navigate and enjoy reading the Bible, preparing for meetings, and using publications.

📱 Select Your Device

Choose your device so the instructions and button locations match your screen:

Device Selected: On Apple iPhone and iPad, the main navigation bar is at the bottom of the screen (Bible, Publications, Meetings, Personal Study).

✅ Preparation Checklist

✓ Connect to Wi-Fi: Connect to home, Kingdom Hall, or library Wi-Fi to avoid mobile data charges.
✓ App Installed: Make sure the app is opened and updated to the latest available version.
✓ Device Storage: Check that your device has at least 2 GB of free space for media downloads.

📖 Lesson 1: Getting Around & Meetings

Lesson Time: 15–20 Mins

Learn how to move between the primary sections of the app, read today's Daily Text, open your meeting study articles directly, and find passages quickly using search and bookmarks.

▶️ View Official Tutorial on JW.ORG

Exercise 1.1: Go to Today's Daily Text

  • 1 Look at the main navigation bar (at the bottom of your screen).
  • 2 Tap Daily Text (☀️).
  • 3 Read today's scripture and the accompanying comment.

Exercise 1.2: Use the Meetings Tab

  • 1 Tap the Meetings tab (📅) on your main navigation bar.
  • 2 Notice the two meeting sections: Midweek Meeting and Weekend Meeting.
  • 3 Tap the Watchtower Study article under the Weekend Meeting to open it immediately.
💡 Tip: The Meetings tab automatically updates every week, so you never have to search for the right issue!

Exercise 1.3 & 1.4: Bookmarks & History

  • 1 Tap Bible (📖) and open any Bible chapter (for example, Matthew chapter 5).
  • 2 Tap the Bookmark icon (🔖) at the top of the screen and choose an open slot to save your place.
  • 3 To return to a verse you looked up earlier, tap the History icon (🕒) to view your recent reading list.

Exercise 1.5: Search for a Scripture

  • 1 Tap the Search icon (🔍) at the top of the screen.
  • 2 Type the phrase: draw close to God (or a phrase of your choice).
  • 3 Tap the search key or result to see matching verses (such as James 4:8).

⬇️ Lesson 2: Downloading & Managing Media

Lesson Time: 15–20 Mins

Learn how to download Bible translations, videos, and publications to your device for reading offline, and how to remove older large files to save storage space.

▶️ View Official Tutorial on JW.ORG

Exercise 2.1: Download an Additional Bible Translation

  • 1 Tap Bible (📖) to see the list of Bible books.
  • 2 Tap the Languages button (🌐) in the upper bar.
  • 3 Look for an available translation with a Cloud icon (☁️) (such as the King James Version) and tap it to download.
  • 4 Once downloaded, the cloud icon disappears, showing it is ready for offline reading.

Exercise 2.2 & 2.3: Download a Video

  • 1 Tap Publications (📚), then tap Videos (or Media).
  • 2 Under Our Meetings and Ministry or Bible Teachings, locate a short introductory video (such as "Why Study the Bible?").
  • 3 Tap the video title and choose a resolution (for example, 720p or 480p for smaller file size) to download.
  • 4 To download in another language, tap the Languages icon on the video page and pick a language (e.g., Spanish).
💡 Data Tip: Choosing 480p or 720p downloads faster and uses significantly less storage space than 1080p.

Exercise 2.4: Check Downloaded Files & Free Up Space

  • 1 Tap Publications (📚), then select the Downloaded section.
  • 2 Change the sort option at the top to Largest Size.
  • 3 Review the files that occupy the most storage space. If there is a large video you no longer watch, tap the Three Dots / More button (⋮) next to it and select Delete.

🖍️ Lesson 3: Highlighting & Personal Study

Lesson Time: 15–20 Mins

Make the material your own by marking answers, adding notes, organizing your study with tags, and creating a safety backup file so you never lose your personal study records.

▶️ View Official Tutorial on JW.ORG

Exercise 3.1 & 3.2: Highlight an Answer

  • 1 Go to the Meetings tab (📅) and open the current week's Watchtower study article.
  • 2 In paragraph 1, press and hold on the first word of an answer, then drag across to highlight it.
  • 3 Choose the Yellow color from the color menu.
  • 4 Tap the highlighted words again to try switching the color (to blue or green), or tap the trash icon to remove the highlight.

Exercise 3.3 & 3.4: Add a Note & Visit Personal Study

  • 1 Highlight another phrase in the article. In the menu that pops up, tap Add Note (📝).
  • 2 Type a brief comment (such as "Good point for comment") and tap Done.
  • 3 Tap the Personal Study tab on your navigation bar.
  • 4 Notice how all your notes, highlights, and tags are neatly collected in this central place!

Exercise 3.5: Back Up Your Personal Study Data

  • 1 Tap Settings (⚙️) on your screen.
  • 2 Tap Backup and Restore.
  • 3 Select Create Backup.
  • 4 Follow the on-screen prompt to save your study backup file (a .jwlibrary file) to your device or cloud drive.
🛡️ Essential Tip: Your notes and highlights are stored locally on your device. Making a backup every few weeks ensures your personal study notes are never lost if you get a new device!
📋 Instructor Class Schedule & Teaching Guide (Click to Expand)

This class can be presented as a comfortable 60-minute session or expanded to 90 minutes with questions and hands-on assistance.

Time Topic Activities & Focus
0:00 – 0:05 Welcome & Screen Orientation Help students locate their navigation bar and adjust text size.
0:05 – 0:25 Lesson 1: Navigation & Meetings Cover Daily Text, Meetings Tab, Bookmarks, History, and Search (Exercises 1.1–1.5).
0:25 – 0:45 Lesson 2: Downloading & Storage Demonstrate Bible and video downloads, and check the Largest Size sort (Exercises 2.1–2.4).
0:45 – 1:00 Lesson 3: Study, Notes & Backup Practice highlighting, personal notes, and create a .jwlibrary backup file (Exercises 3.1–3.5).

Teaching Suggestions:

  • Encourage students to master one simple action at a time before moving forward.
  • Pair students with a study partner who can assist if someone gets stuck on a menu.
  • Remind everyone to connect to the venue's Wi-Fi network before downloading videos.
