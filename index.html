JW Library Tutorial

A practical one-hour tutorial to help you navigate and enjoy reading the Bible, preparing for Christian meetings, and using Bible-based publications.

📱 Select Your Device

Choose your device so the instructions and button locations match your screen:

Device Selected: On Apple iPhone and iPad, the main navigation bar is at the bottom of the screen (Bible, Publications, Meetings, Personal Study).

✅ Preparation Checklist

✓ Early Arrival: Arrive 10–15 minutes early so helpers can verify your Wi-Fi and make sure your Bible and Daily Text are downloaded.
✓ Emergency Silencing: If sound ever plays unexpectedly, press and hold the physical Volume Down button on the side of your phone/tablet, press the Mute key (🔇) on your keyboard, or tap Pause (⏸️).
✓ Battery: Arrive with your device fully charged (or bring your charging cord).
✓ Screen Timeout: Set auto-lock or screen timeout to 2 or 5 minutes so the screen stays on during class.
✓ Wi-Fi Connected: Connect to venue Wi-Fi so you can download study materials and preserve mobile data.
✓ Free Space: Ensure your device has at least 2 GB of storage for downloads.

📖 Lesson 1: Finding Scriptures & Meetings

Time: 15 Mins

Learn how to move between the primary sections of the app, make reading comfortable by enlarging the text, open any Bible chapter, and jump directly to your weekly meeting materials.

▶️ View Official Tutorial on JW.ORG

Core 1.1: Daily Text & Making Text Bigger

  • 1 Look at the main navigation bar (at the bottom of your screen) and tap Daily Text (☀️).
  • 2 Look at the top bar and tap the Appearance / Text Size icon (A A).
  • 3 Drag the slider to the right to make the print larger and easy to read.
💡 Reading Tip: Once you set your preferred text size here, the app remembers it across all Bible chapters and articles.

Core 1.2: Open a Bible Book & Chapter

  • 1 Tap Bible (📖) on your main navigation bar.
  • 2 Tap a Bible book (for example, Matthew).
  • 3 Tap chapter 5 to begin reading.

Core 1.3: Use the Meetings Tab

  • 1 Tap the Meetings tab (📅) on your navigation bar.
  • 2 Notice the two weekly meeting sections: Midweek Meeting and Weekend Meeting.
  • 3 Tap the Watchtower Study article under the Weekend Meeting to open it immediately with one tap.

Instructor Demonstration: Bookmarks, History & Search

The instructor will show how to set a Bookmark (🔖), revisit recent verses using History (🕒), and search for words with Search (🔍). You can practice these at home!

⬇️ Lesson 2: Downloading and Using Media

Time: 15 Mins

Understand how offline reading works, download publications on Wi-Fi to preserve your mobile cellular data, and enjoy listening to audio recordings read aloud.

▶️ View Official Tutorial on JW.ORG

Core 2.1: Wi-Fi Check & Mobile Data Safety

  • 1 Look at the top of your device screen to confirm the Wi-Fi icon is connected.
  • 2 Why this matters: Downloading while on Wi-Fi ensures you never incur mobile cellular data charges.
  • 3 Offline Peace of Mind: Once downloaded, publications and audio stay on your device forever—no internet or cellular signal is needed at meetings!

Core 2.2: Download an Item for Offline Use

  • 1 Tap Publications (📚), or go to the Meetings tab (📅).
  • 2 Look for an item marked with a Cloud icon (☁️) (e.g., this month's meeting workbook or a short video).
  • 3 Tap the item to download it. When the cloud icon disappears, the item is completely saved on your device for offline reading.

Core 2.3: Listen with Audio & Emergency Quick-Silencing

  • 1 Open today's Daily Text or any Bible chapter.
  • 2 Tap the Headphones / Play button (🎧) at the top of the screen to start the reading aloud.
  • 3 Practice Emergency Silencing: Stop the sound immediately by tapping Pause (⏸️) on the screen, or by pressing the physical Volume Down button on the side of your device (or the Mute key on a computer keyboard).
🔇 Peace of Mind Tip: Never fear media playing unexpectedly. On a phone or tablet, holding the side Volume Down button is your universal emergency brake. On a laptop or PC, press the keyboard Mute key (🔇) or click Pause (⏸️).

Instructor Demonstration: Managing Storage Space

The instructor will demonstrate how to go to Publications > Downloaded, sort by Largest Size, and delete older videos when device storage space runs low.

🖍️ Lesson 3: Personal Study Tools

Time: 15 Mins

Learn how to mark answers for the meeting, change or remove highlights with ease, and create a safety backup file so years of your personal study markings are never lost.

▶️ View Official Tutorial on JW.ORG

Core 3.1: Highlight an Answer in Yellow

  • 1 Tap the Meetings tab (📅) and open the upcoming Watchtower study article.
  • 2 In paragraph 1, press and hold on the first word of an answer, then gently drag your finger across the sentence.
  • 3 Tap the Yellow circle in the color menu that appears. Your answer is highlighted!

Core 3.2: Change Color or Remove a Highlight

  • 1 Tap directly on the highlighted yellow text you just made.
  • 2 Tap another color (like blue or green) to change it.
  • 3 To erase a highlight completely, tap the Trash can icon.

Core 3.3: Create a Safety Backup File

  • 1 Tap Settings (⚙️) on your screen.
  • 2 Tap Backup and Restore.
  • 3 Tap Create Backup.
  • 4 Follow the on-screen prompt to save your study backup file (a .jwlibrary file).
🛡️ Peace of Mind Tip: Your notes and highlights are saved on your physical device. Creating a backup file every month ensures your study notes are protected if you ever get a new phone or tablet.

Instructor Demonstration: Notes, Tags & Personal Study Hub

The instructor will show how you can attach written notes (📝) to verses and view all your collected bookmarks and highlights in the Personal Study tab.

📋 Facilitator & Trainer Guide (60-Minute Master Plan)

This master schedule dedicates 15 minutes to each lesson with a built-in 10-minute wrap-up buffer for a relaxed, pressure-free session.

Time Session Core Focus & Hands-On Milestones
0:00 – 0:05 Welcome & Orientation (5m) Quick Bible readiness check (helpers assist anyone needing downloads), peace-of-mind emergency silencing tip (side Volume Down on phones/tablets; Mute key on PC), and screen timeout check.
0:05 – 0:20 Lesson 1: Finding Scriptures & Meetings (15m) Core 1.1: Daily Text & Text Size (A A) • Core 1.2: Bible Book/Chapter • Core 1.3: Meetings tab.
0:20 – 0:35 Lesson 2: Downloading and Using Media (15m) Core 2.1: Wi-Fi data safety • Core 2.2: Download for offline use • Core 2.3: Audio player (🎧) & emergency quick-silence.
0:35 – 0:50 Lesson 3: Personal Study Tools (15m) Core 3.1: Highlighting in yellow • Core 3.2: Adjusting color/erasing • Core 3.3: Creating backup file.
0:50 – 1:00 Wrap-Up, Review & Questions (10m) Review accomplishments, answer questions, and utilize flex time if earlier exercises ran over.

Key Facilitator Recommendations:

  • Pre-Class Setup Clinic (15 Mins Early): Announce that doors open 15 minutes early so helpers can connect attendees to venue Wi-Fi and verify the Bible and Daily Text are downloaded before the first minute of class.
  • Emergency Silencing ("Panic Button") First: Show students the physical Volume Down button and Pause icon right at the start. Knowing they can instantly silence sound removes tech anxiety before opening media.
  • Table Assistants (Helpers): Arrange for 1 or 2 helpers to quietly circulate. When a student accidentally leaves an app, a helper can tap them back in seconds without stopping the class.
  • The 10-Minute Flex Buffer: If an exercise takes a little longer, calmly borrow 3–5 minutes from the 10-minute wrap-up buffer without rushing.
  • Stay-After Clinic: Announce that the instructor and helpers will remain for 15 minutes after class for anyone wanting one-on-one help.
  • Physical Handouts (When Beneficial): Having printed copies of the 1-page Class Program on the tables allows learners to follow all exercise steps immediately, without needing to load this website on mobile data.
  • Table QR Cards & Screen Projection (Optional): Displaying a QR code on the screen or placing printed Table Cards (PDF) on tables lets learners open the guide with a single camera tap without typing URLs.
  • Add to Home Screen (Offline Tip): Show learners how to use "Share > Add to Home Screen" (iPhone) or "Menu > Add to Home screen" (Android). Important: Open the new icon once while online to complete caching. Afterward, this practice guide will open and work completely offline without an internet connection (though external videos, PDFs, and JW.ORG links still need internet).
