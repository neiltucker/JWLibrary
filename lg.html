Obunene bw'Ennukuta:

Okuyiga Okukozesa JW Library

Okuyiga okw'essaawa emu okw'omugaso okukuyamba okukozesa apu eno n'okunyumirwa okusoma Bayibuli, okutegeka enkuŋŋaana z'Ekikristaayo, n'okukozesa ebitabo ebyesigamiziddwa ku Bayibuli.

📱 Londa ekika ky'Essimu, Tabbuleeti, oba Kompyuta gy'Okozesa

Londa ekika ky'essimu, tabbuleeti, oba kompyuta gy'okozesa, londa ku biragiro ebikwatagana ne biri ku sikuliini yo:

Ky'Olonze: Ku iPhone ne iPad za Apple, olukalala olukulu olw'okutambuliramu luli wansi ku sikuliini (Bayibuli, Ebitabo, Enkuŋŋaana, Okwesomesa).

✅ Olukalala lw'Okwetegeka

✓ Tuuka nga Bukyali: Tuuka eddakiika 10–15 nga bukyali abayambi basobole okukebera Wi-Fi yo n'okukakasa nti Bayibuli yo n'Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku biwanuddwa.
✓ Okusirisa amaloboozi: Ssinga eddoboozi livaayo nga terisuubiddwa, nyiga era onyweze eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi eriri ku mabbali g'essimu oba tabbuleeti yo, nyiga eppeesa erisirisa (🔇) ku kibodi yo, oba nyiga Yimiriza (⏸️).
✓ Bbaatule: Jangu n'essimu, tabbuleeti, oba kompyuta yo nga bbaatule yaayo ejjudde (oba leeta waya yo ecaajinga).
✓ Ekiseera sikuliini mw'Ezikirira: Teekako ddakiika 2 oba 5 sikuliini esigale ng'eyaka mu kiseera kya kiraasi.
✓ Wi-Fi Eyungiddwa: Yunga ku Wi-Fi y'ekifo osobole okuwanula ebintu eby'okuyiga n'okukekkereza data w'essimu.
✓ Ekifo Ekitaliiko Kintu: Kakasa nti essimu yo, tabbuleeti, oba kompyuta yo erina waakiri GB 2 ez'ekifo eky'okuterekamu fayiro ez'okuwanulwa.

📖 Essomo 1: Okuzuula Ebyawandiikibwa n'Enkuŋŋaana

Ekiseera: Eddakiika 15

Yiga engeri y'okuva mu kitundu ekimu ekikulu eky'apu okugenda mu kirala, okusoma mu ngeri ennyangu ng'ogaziya ennukuta, okuggulawo essuula yonna eya Bayibuli, n'okugenda butereevu ku bintu by'enkuŋŋaana zo eza buli wiiki.

▶️ Laba Obulagirizi Obutongole ku JW.ORG (mu Lungereza)

Okwegezaamu 1.1: Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku n'Okugaziya Ennukuta

  • 1 Tunuulira olukalala olukulu olw'okutambuliramu (wansi ku sikuliini yo) olwo onyige Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku (☀️) (Daily Text).
  • 2 Tunuulira olukalala olwa waggulu onyige akabonero k'Endabika / Obunene bw'Ennukuta (A A).
  • 3 Sereza akasaaliro ku ddyo ennukuta zifuuke nnene era nnyangu okusoma.
💡 Amagezi ag'Okusoma: Bw'omala okuteekawo obunene bw'ennukuta bw'oyagala wano, apu ebujjukira mu ssuula zonna eza Bayibuli ne mu bitundu byonna.

Okwegezaamu 1.2: Ggulawo Ekitabo n'Essuula ya Bayibuli

  • 1 Nyiga Bayibuli (📖) (Bible) ku lukalala lwo olukulu olw'okutambuliramu.
  • 2 Nyiga ekitabo kya Bayibuli (okugeza, Matayo).
  • 3 Nyiga essuula 5 otandike okusoma.

Okwegezaamu 1.3: Kozesa Ekitundu ky'Enkuŋŋaana

  • 1 Nyiga ekitundu ky'Enkuŋŋaana (📅) (Meetings) ku lukalala lwo olw'okutambuliramu.
  • 2 Weetegereze ebitundu ebibiri eby'enkuŋŋaana eza buli wiiki: olukuŋŋaana olwa wakati mu wiiki (Midweek) n'olukuŋŋaana olwa wiikendi (Weekend).
  • 3 Nyiga ekitundu ky'Omunaala gw'Omukuumi ekisomebwa mu kibiina (Watchtower Study) ekiri wansi w'olukuŋŋaana olwa wiikendi okukiggulawo amangu ddala ng'onyize omulundi gumu.

Okulaga kw’omusomesa: Bookmark, History, ne Noonya

Omusomesa ajja kulaga engeri y'okuteekawo akabonero akalaga w'okomye okusoma (🔖) (Bookmark), okuddayo amangu ku nnyiriri z'obadde osoma ng'onyiga History (🕒), n'okunoonya ebigambo ng'okozesa Noonya (🔍) (Search). Osobola okubyegezaamu ng'oli waka!

⬇️ Essomo 2: Okuwanula n'Okukozesa Amaloboozi ne Vidiyo

Ekiseera: Eddakiika 15

Tegeera engeri okusoma awatali Intaneeti gye kukolamu, wanula ebitabo ng'oli ku Wi-Fi okukekkereza data w'essimu yo, era onyumirwe okuwuliriza amaloboozi agasoma mu ddoboozi.

▶️ Laba Obulagirizi Obutongole ku JW.ORG (mu Lungereza)

Okwegezaamu 2.1: Okukebera Wi-Fi n'Okukuuma Data w'Essimu

  • 1 Tunuulira waggulu ku sikuliini yo okakase nti akabonero ka Wi-Fi kalaga nti oyungiddwa.
  • 2 Lwaki kino kikulu: Okuwanula ng'oli ku Wi-Fi kikakasa nti tosasula data w'essimu.
  • 3 Emirembe Awatali Intaneeti: Ebitabo n'amaloboozi bwe bimala okuwanulwa, bisigala mu ssimu yo, tabbuleeti, oba kompyuta yo ennaku zonna—teweetaaga Intaneeti wadde akabonero k’omukutu gwe ssimu mu nkuŋŋaana!

Okwegezaamu 2.2: Wanula Ekintu Okukikozesa Awatali Intaneeti

  • 1 Nyiga Ebitabo (📚) (Publications), oba genda mu kitundu ky'Enkuŋŋaana (📅) (Meetings).
  • 2 Noonya ekintu ekiriko akabonero k'Ekire (☁️) (okugeza, Akatabo k'Enkuŋŋaana ak'omwezi guno oba vidiyo empi).
  • 3 Nyiga ekintu ekyo okukiwanula. Akabonero k'ekire bwe kaggwaawo, ekintu ekyo kiba kiterekeddwa kyonna ku ssimu yo, tabbuleeti, oba kompyuta yo osobole okukisoma awatali Intaneeti.

Okwegezaamu 2.3: Wuliriza Amaloboozi n’Engeri ey’Okuzikiza Amaloboozi Mangu

  • 1 Ggulawo Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku eky'olwaleero oba essuula yonna eya Bayibuli.
  • 2 Nyiga eppeesa lya Headphones / Zannya (🎧) (Play) eriri waggulu ku sikuliini okutandika okuwuliriza okusoma.
  • 3 Weegezeemu Okusirisa Amaloboozi: Komya eddoboozi amangu ddala ng'onyiga Yimiriza (⏸️) ku sikuliini, oba ng'onyiga eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi eriri ku mabbali g'essimu oba tabbuleeti yo (oba eppeesa erisirisa ku kibodi kya kompyuta).
🔇 Amagezi ag'Emirembe: Totya nti amaloboozi oba vidiyo binaavaayo nga tebisuubiddwa! Ku ssimu oba tabbuleeti, nyweza eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi eriri ku mabbali. Ku laputopu oba kompyuta, nyiga eppeesa erisirisa (🔇) ku kibodi oba nyiga Yimiriza (⏸️).

Okulaga kw’Omusomesa: Okulabirira Ekifo ky’Okutereka Fayiro

Omusomesa ajja kulaga engeri y'okugenda mu Ebitabo > Ebiwanuddwa (Publications > Downloaded), okusengeka fayiro ng'osinziira ku Esinga Obunene (Largest Size), n'okusangulamu vidiyo enkadde ng'ekifo eky'okuterekamu ku ssimu yo, tabbuleeti, oba kompyuta yo nga kitandise okukendeera.

🖍️ Essomo 3: Ebikozesebwa mu Kwesomesa

Ekiseera: Eddakiika 15

Yiga engeri y'okussaako langi ku by'okuddamu eby'olukuŋŋaana, okukyusa oba okuggyawo langi mu ngeri ennyangu, n’okukola fayiro ey’okutereka kkopi ey’obukuumi, kisobozese okunoonyereza n’okusoma kwo okw'emyaka emingi obutafiirwa.

▶️ Laba Obulagirizi Obutongole ku JW.ORG (mu Lungereza)

Okwegezaamu 3.1: Ssaako Langi ya Kyenvu ku Ky'okuddamu

  • 1 Nyiga ekitundu ky'Enkuŋŋaana (📅) (Meetings) oggulewo ekitundu ky'Omunaala gw'Omukuumi ekinaddako okusomebwa mu kibiina.
  • 2 Mu katundu 1, nyiga era onyweze ekigambo ekisooka mu ky'okuddamu, olwo owalule mpola engalo yo ku sentensi eyo.
  • 3 Nyiga akatundu aka Kyenvu (Yellow) mu menu ya langi evaayo. Eky'okuddamu kyo kissiddwako langi!

Okwegezaamu 3.2: Kyusa Langi oba Ggyawo Langi

  • 1 Nyiga butereevu ku bigambo by'osizzaako langi ya kyenvu kati.
  • 2 Nyiga langi endala (nga bbulu oba kiragala) okugikyusa.
  • 3 Okuggyawo langi yonna, nyiga akabonero ka Kasasiro.

Okwegezaamu 3.3: Kola Kkopi Ya Fayiro

  • 1 Nyiga By'Oyagala (⚙️) (Settings) ku sikuliini yo.
  • 2 Nyiga Okutereka Kkopi n'Okugizzaawo (Backup and Restore).
  • 3 Nyiga Kola Kkopi (Create Backup).
  • 4 Goberera ebiragiro by'olaba ku sikuliini okutereka kkopi ya fayiro ey'okusoma (fayiro ya .jwlibrary).
🛡️ Amagezi ag'Emirembe: Ebiwandiiko byo ne langi z'osizzaako biterekebwa ku ssimu yo, tabbuleeti, oba kompyuta yo. Okukola kkopi ya fayiro buli mwezi kikakasa nti ebiwandiiko byo eby'okusoma bikuumibwa singa ofuna essimu oba tabbuleeti empya.

Okulaga kw’Omusomesa: Ebiwandiiko, Ttaaga, n'Ekitundu ky'Okwesomesa

Omusomesa ajja kulaga engeri gy'osobola okugatta ebiwandiiko by'owandiise (📝) ku nnyiriri n'okulaba obubonero bwo bwonna obulaga w'okomye okusoma (Bookmarks) ne langi zonna mu kitundu ky'Okwesomesa (Personal Study).

Amagezi Amakulu eri Akulembera Kiraasi:

  • Okuyambibwa nga Kiraasi Tennatandika (Eddakiika 15 nga Bukyali): Langirira nti enzigi zijja kuggulwawo eddakiika 15 nga bukyali abayambi basobole okuyunga abazze ku Wi-Fi y'ekifo n'okukakasa nti Bayibuli n'Ekyawandiikibwa eky'Olunaku biwanuddwa nga eddakiika esooka eya kiraasi tennatuuka.
  • Okusirisa amaloboozi ("Eppeesa erisirisa") Kusooka: Laga abayizi eppeesa erikendeeza eddoboozi eriri ku mabbali n'akabonero ka Yimiriza ku ntandikwa ddala. Bwe bamanya nti basobola okusirisa eddoboozi amangu, okutya kwabwe ku tekinologiya kuggwaawo nga tebannaggulawo maloboozi ne vidiyo.
  • Abayambi ku Mmeeza: Tegeka abayambi 1 oba 2 batambuletambule mpola. Omuyizi bw'ava mu apu mu butanwa, omuyambi asobola okumuzzaayo mu sikonda ntono awatali kuyimiriza kiraasi.
  • Eddakiika 10 ez'Enyongeza: Okwegezaamu bwe kutwala ekiseera ekisingako, kwata mpola eddakiika 3–5 okuva mu ddakiika 10 ez'okufundikira awatali kwanguyiriza.
  • Okuyambibwa Oluvannyuma lwa Kiraasi: Langirira nti omusomesa n'abayambi bajja kusigalawo eddakiika 15 oluvannyuma lwa kiraasi okuyamba oyo yenna ayagala okuyambibwa ng'ali yekka.
  • Kkopi Ezikubiddwa ku mpapula (Bwe kiba kyetaagisa): Okussa kkopi z'Enteekateeka ya Kiraasi ey'olupapula olumu ku mmeeza kisobozesa abayizi okugoberera emitendera gyonna egy'okwegezaamu mbagirawo, awatali kwetaaga kuggulawo mukutu guno ku ddata ey'essimu.
  • Koodi za QR ku Mmeeza oba ku lutimbe (Ssi kya buwaze): Bwe kiba kisoboka, okussa koodi ya QR ku lutimbe oba okuteeka Obukadi bw'oku mmeeza (PDF) kiyamba abayizi okuggulawo ekitabo kino mbagirawo nga bakozesa kamera y'essimu yaabwe awatali kuwandiika linnya lya mukutu.
  • Yongereza ku Sikuliini Ey'omu Maaso (Amagezi g'Okukozesa nga Tewali Intaneeti): Laga abayizi engeri y'okukozesa "Share > Add to Home Screen" (iPhone) oba "Menu > Add to Home screen" (Android).
